What this is, and what it is not

The LOFT assessment is a face-to-face interview. You will be asked to demonstrate and describe — out loud, in your own words, to an assessment team. Multiple choice cannot test that, and passing this does not mean you are ready.

What it is good for is the factual half: approval levels, thresholds, timeframes, who signs what. Those are the things people lose marks on, and they are exactly what drilling fixes. Use this to get the facts solid, then practise saying the full answers aloud from the study guide.

Answers marked asset come from Hebron/Hibernia local material, which no source document in the repository can confirm. They are being reviewed. Treat them as current practice, not as verified fact.
